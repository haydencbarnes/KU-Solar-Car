Press & coverage

Public articles and write-ups about KU Solar Car, compiled from web search in September 2026. This is not the team blog — those posts are in the news archive. Links go to the original publishers and may break.

News coverage

  • Solar Car club loses West Campus headquarters after negotiations failed with KU mechanical engineering
    KJHK 90.7 FM · Abdullah Al-Awhad · 2022-08-15

    Campus radio report on KU withdrawing shop space, insurance, and advising after 2022 FSGP. Cites reporting first gathered by the Open Kansan; that original piece was not still online.

  • Solar Powered Cars Circle the Track at Heartland Motorsports Park
    WIBW · 2021-07-30

    Topeka TV piece on FSGP 2021. Notes that KU had accepted the challenge, then exited and did not race that year.

  • KU Solar Car team unveils progress on car and prepares for race

    Astra chassis and arrays shown on the LEEP2 lawn. Team then aiming for the 2021 Formula Sun Grand Prix in Topeka. Names Michael Srimongkolkul, Zachary Hall, Lance Erickson, Zack Howard, and Mackenzie Marco.

  • KU Solar Car integrates Schools of Business and Engineering with business sub-team
    KU School of Business · 2019-12-05

    How non-engineering students handled budget, sponsors, and LaunchKU for the team.

  • Solar car team at KU aims to build a vehicle and race by next summer
    Lawrence Journal-World · Jackson Barton · 2019-07-21

    Early profile after the team visited FSGP 2019 in Austin. About 25 members, first-car fundraising, and the “one lap” goal. Names Michael Srimongkolkul, Nick Kellerman, Hayden Barnes, and Gabe Owings.
