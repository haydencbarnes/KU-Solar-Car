Getting Started

Why join

KU Solar Car?​

Broader Horizons

KU Solar Car is an opportunity for you to work in a multi-disciplinary team, network, gain technical and business skills, and, of course, awesome friendships! The more you get involved, the more you will gain!

Advocate Sustainable Technology

If you care about the environment, KU Solar Car is the right team for you! We are here learning and practicing renewables, zero emission transportation technologies, and so much more!

Build Your Professional Portfolio

Being involved in KU Solar Car shows potential employers that you are eager to learn and can handle multiple responsibilities. Some of our alumni have worked and interned at NASA, Pinterest, Google, Snapchat, Garmin, Amazon, Cerner, The Spaceship Company, etc.!

You Get to Travel For Races!

Travel with your friends for Solar Car races; Formula Sun Grand Prix and the American Solar Challenge! You'll meet teams from all over the world and build lasting relationships. Join us on this journey and build a solar car!
