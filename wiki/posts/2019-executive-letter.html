2019 Executive Review Letter

Published: 2019-05-24

Hi Everyone!

Over the last year, KU Solar Car has been bustling with activity and has made a lot of progress. We have also faced many challenges and adversity that have strengthened our team. As program director, I’ve been fortunate to work with the fantastic people on our team to accomplish our goals and mission. We’ve reached some significant milestones: found a manufacturing space for building the car, got funding for our electrical systems, and are approved to reach out to companies for in-kind and monetary donations. As the pioneering team, we want to leave something special here at KU that will last longer and leave a remarkable impact. Big thanks to everyone who believes in us and support us. We will continue to work hard over the Summer.

Sincerely,

Michael Srimongkolkul

Program Director

-Edited by Hayden Barnes - KUSC Business Lead
