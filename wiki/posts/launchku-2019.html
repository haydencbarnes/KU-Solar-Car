LaunchKU 2019

Published: 2019-10-28

KU Solar Car's first LaunchKU fundraising project began last week. Our crowdfunding goal is to raise $10,000 for race entry registration, electrical components, and mechanical parts for the first-ever KU Solar Car.

To help, all you have to do is click here [ launchku.org/kusolarcar ] . From the LaunchKU page, you can donate, share, or read more about our fundraising project. Thank you in advance for your generosity and time; we appreciate it. As always, every little bit helps!

// Hayden Barnes // KU Solar Car Business Lead
